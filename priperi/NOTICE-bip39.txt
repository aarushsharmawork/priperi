The word list and entropy/checksum format follow BIP39. Priperi uses them only to
encode a fresh random one-time transfer secret, not a cryptocurrency wallet seed.
BIP39 specifies the MIT license: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki

BIP39 authors: Marek Palatinus, Pavol Rusnak, Aaron Voisine, Sean Bowe.
